About the Programme

SpaceCraft is Gopal Sharma Memorial School's space-observation initiative for curious young minds. Through guided night-sky viewings, telescope sessions and a specially curated film library, students explore the origins of the cosmos — from the Big Bang to the birth of our own planet — and learn to see the night sky not just as stars, but as a story billions of years in the making.
