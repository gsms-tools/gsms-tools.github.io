How does a search engine
find anything... instantly?

Meet Pixel, a friendly search robot who will show you how the internet gets organized, searched, and sorted — all in a few blinks of an eye!

What IS a search engine, anyway?

Think of the whole internet as a GIGANTIC library with billions of books — but with no librarian and no order at all!

A search engine is like a super-fast librarian. It already knows where every single book (website) is, what's written inside each one, and which books answer your question best — so when you ask it something, it hands you the right books in under a second!

Step 1 — Crawling: Pixel explores the web

Tiny robot helpers called "crawlers" zoom from website to website, following links like stepping stones, peeking inside every page they find.

Step 2 — Indexing: filing it all away

Every page Pixel reads gets turned into a neat little index card and dropped into a giant, organized filing cabinet called the index.

Step 3 — Ranking: who goes first?

When thousands of pages match your question, Pixel scores each one to decide who deserves the top spot.
